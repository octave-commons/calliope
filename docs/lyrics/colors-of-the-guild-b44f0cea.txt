Title: Colors of the Guild / ギルドの色
ID: 8b075c0a-e8cb-4dd8-8068-18a35a0b2d08
Prompt:
## OVERTURE — Ruins at Dawn

*(soft strings; distant water; low organ)*

The dust is settling in the light.
No banners raised.
No wrong, no right.

霧はまだ流れる。

## VERSE I — The Weight

I asked for justice.
I called for flame.

I thought that standing
meant the same.

But standing burns
if left alone.

A spine of steel
can turn to stone.

I see it now.

## PRE-CHORUS — Quiet Choir

*(choir hum builds softly)*

Not every rebel
wears a crown.

Not every victory
brings one down.

## CHORUS — Colors of the Guild

*(strings swell gently; choir joins)*

I wear the colors of the Guild.

Not as conquest.
Not as shield.

ギルドの色
背に受けて

I chose the frame
that must be held.

No anthem thunder.
No parade.

Just hands that steady
what we made.

## VERSE II — Bitterness

It would be easier
if they cheered.

If someone named me
what I feared—

A hero.

But the word dissolves.

This is not legend.
This is resolve.

証は
継続。

## BRIDGE — Echo of the Queen

*(harpsichord hints at Aquila motif, softened; soprano hum above)*

Soprano (distant):
Aquila—

*(melody lowered, gentle)*

Rebel:
I heard you.

I did.

But I will not rise
just to be seen.

## FINAL CHORUS — Integration

*(choir fuller; still restrained; no explosive percussion)*

I wear the colors of the Guild.

Not because I was chosen—

Because I chose
what must be built.

ギルドの色
静かに光る。

If justice comes,
let it be quiet.

If power stands,
let it be shared.

No throne required.

Just breath repaired.

## CODA — Steady State

*(music thins; waterfall clearer)*

The sanctuary holds.

The system breathes.

I remain.

息。
