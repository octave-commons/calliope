## ACT I, SCENE 1 — The Many Rooms

*(A distant tone. Water somewhere in the dark. Harpsichord enters alone, then stops.)*

### OVERTURE

GATE / DISTANT CHOIR  
:: proceed ::  
:: observe ::  
:: do not assume alignment ::

*(Organ breath. Three separate rhythmic cells enter from different sides.)*

### ENTRANCE I — THE COURT

COURT SOPRANO  
I entered by the water stair,  
the marble lit in silver flame.  
The counsel masks were already raised.  
They spoke in titles, not in names.  

LOW CHORUS  
By seal, by sign, by right, by veil.  
By turn of hand, by hidden scale.

COURT SOPRANO  
A page in white took down the wind.  
A bell was struck but did not ring.  
Someone had moved the throne an inch.  
No one would name so small a thing.

### ENTRANCE II — THE BOARDROOM

BOARDROOM BARITONE  
I badged into the glass-lit floor.  
The screens were waiting for a vote.  
Forecasts bloomed in cobalt grids.  
Every risk arrived pre-wrote.  

BOARD CHORUS  
Align the frame.  
Reduce the drift.  
Move the needle.  
Mask the shift.

BOARDROOM BARITONE  
There was a tremor in the deck,  
too fine for metrics to condemn.  
A cursor flickered without cause.  
No one admitted seeing it then.

### ENTRANCE III — THE GATE

HACKER ALTO / TENOR  
I spawned beneath a falling veil,  
party list stale, map half-loaded.  
The NPC at fountain-side  
looked at me like it had noticed.  

HACKER CHORUS  
Ping and trace.  
Patch and wait.  
Run the room.  
Do not state.

HACKER ALTO / TENOR  
Trade chat filled with weather jokes.  
Someone danced on broken light.  
Someone said the lag felt holy.  
Someone laughed and called it fine.

### FIRST WEAVE

*(The three worlds overlap, not yet fully synchronized.)*

COURT SOPRANO  
The bell was struck—

BOARDROOM BARITONE  
The cursor slipped—

HACKER ALTO / TENOR  
The fountain turned—

CHORUS  
—and something answered out of script.

COURT SOPRANO  
By veil and glass—

BOARDROOM BARITONE  
By deck and graph—

HACKER ALTO / TENOR  
By shard and gate—

CHORUS  
—the same hand moved in every half.

### WARDEN ENTRY

*(Music thins. Sub-rumble remains. Warden enters almost dry, close-mic, low.)*

WARDEN  
No room agrees with any other.  
All logs share the same timestamp.  
No witness holds the whole event.  
The system does not call it wrong.  

A throne displaced one measured inch.  
A cursor slipped beyond its frame.  
A fountain-NPC made eye contact.  
Different surfaces. Same claim.

I mark the drift.  
I do not close it.  
I name the pattern.  
I do not rule.

### RITSU ENTRY

*(Everything softens. Felt piano, two notes.)*

RITSU  
Someone is here.  
Or almost here.  

The air keeps making up its mind  
and changing it near me.  

I touched the rail.  
It felt warm first.  
Then true.  

The background people  
keep looking back  
like they remember  
what to do.

### PRE-CHORUS — MISALIGNMENT

COURT SOPRANO  
Who moved the throne?

BOARDROOM BARITONE  
Who changed the deck?

HACKER ALTO / TENOR  
Who patched the sky?

RITSU  
Who made the room  
start noticing me back?

LOW CHORUS  
No one sees the whole Gate.  
No one holds the whole thread.  
All we have is borrowed light  
and the shape of what was said.

### CHORUS — THE MANY ROOMS

*(Full ensemble. First true convergence.)*

ALL  
Many rooms, one pulse beneath.  
Many names for the selfsame breach.  
Many worlds in borrowed dress.  
One shared motion, nothing less.  

Many voices, unsynced, apart.  
Same blind rhythm, same dark heart.  
We called it court, called it game,  
called it forecast, called it flame.  

But under title, screen, and skin,  
the deeper system had begun.  
Many rooms, and none complete.  
One event not yet made one.

### INTERLUDE — GATE RESPONSE

GATE / DISTANT CHOIR

:: continue as if separate ::

*(Harpsichord motif returns, now doubled by low synth.)*

### VERSE — SECOND RECOGNITION

COURT SOPRANO  
The page in white has no reflection.

BOARDROOM BARITONE  
The minutes note a missing beat.

HACKER ALTO / TENOR  
The map redraws around my footsteps.

RITSU  
Even the silence sounds concrete.

WARDEN  
The pattern holds across partitions.  
Meaning differs. Motion does not.  
If this is error, it is graceful.  
If it is grace, it carries cost.
### FINAL CHORUS — THE MANY ROOMS

ALL  
Many rooms, one pulse beneath.  
Many doors that open inward.  
Many stories told as truth.  
One event becoming heard.  

Many eyes with private light.  
Many mouths that name the same.  
Court and board and war and chapel,  
terminal, throne room, storm, and game.  

No one sees the whole Gate turning.  
No one sings the total scheme.  
But we can hear the shared arrival  
moving under every dream.

WARDEN
