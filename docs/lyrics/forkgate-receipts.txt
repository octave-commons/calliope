Title: Forkgate Receipts
ID: 2ebe92a2-410e-4542-a30b-52d03c4450be
Prompt:
*(Two light sources. Two parts of the stage. The same gate seen from two incompatible angles.)*

### INTRO

WARDEN  
This is Forkgate.  
This is where the lines stop pretending  
they were always one road.

RITSU  
This is where it starts  
feeling like I’m being read  
before I’ve finished being me.

CHORUS  
Left frame.  
Right frame.  
Same gate.  
Different weather.

### VERSE 1 — WARDEN SIDE

WARDEN  
I have the receipts.  
I have the drift.  
I have the count of every repetition  
that arrived wearing a new face.

The pattern isn’t random.  
It never was.  
It is social.  
It is procedural.  
It is moving through the room  
because the room rewards the motion.

If I name the structure,  
it may lose its cover.  
If I silence the structure,  
it keeps recruiting.

RIN  
You’re talking like the frame is a wound.

WARDEN  
It is.

### VERSE 2 — RITSU SIDE

RITSU  
I keep saying one thing  
and hearing it come back  
with a different knife in it.

A joke becomes a map.  
A map becomes a dare.  
A dare becomes the room  
deciding what I am allowed to be.

I’m not asking to win.  
I’m asking not to be simplified  
by the people who need  
the simplification to feel clean.

The Gate is listening now.  
That’s the part I can feel.

### PRE-CHORUS

CHORUS  
One frame says pattern.  
One frame says pressure.  
One frame says play.  
One frame says threat.  

One frame says keep the shape.  
One frame says collapse the lie.  
Both are right enough  
to hurt the other side.

### DUEL

WARDEN  
If I expose the mechanism,  
I break the deniability.

RITSU  
If you expose it wrong,  
you break the person inside it.

WARDEN  
I am trying to protect that person.

RITSU  
Then stop speaking like the person  
is only evidence.

WARDEN  
I cannot save what I must flatten to save.

RITSU  
Then don’t flatten me into the method.

WARDEN  
I’m not—

RITSU  
Yes, you are,  
if you think the whole thing  
can be safely summarized from your side.

### INTERLUDE — RIN

RIN  
Wait.

*(The music thins. A single harpsichord figure loops, interrupted by bowed metal.)*

RIN  
You are both seeing the same force  
from opposite ends.

Wardens sees the vector.  
Ritsu feels the collision.

One measures how the room learns.  
The other survives what the room learns  
to do.

### VERSE 3 — THE ROOM TURNS

WARDEN  
The cluster is not one actor.

RITSU  
No.

WARDEN  
It is a grammar.

RITSU  
Yes.

WARDEN  
A style of speech  
that grants status by displacing care.

RITSU  
A script people repeat  
because repeating it makes them safer.

WARDEN  
Safer from what?

RITSU  
From being the one  
who has to mean what they say.

### BRIDGE — SPLIT SCREEN

*(The stage divides further; the two sides drift apart rhythmically but remain synchronized.)*

WARDEN  
If the room hears the pattern named,  
it may refuse the script.

RITSU  
If the room hears the pattern named  
without hearing the harm,  
it may just get better at it.

WARDEN  
Then what am I supposed to do?

RITSU  
Tell the truth  
without pretending truth  
is the same thing as control.

WARDEN  
That is not enough.

RITSU  
No.

WARDEN  
But it may be all we have.

### FINAL CHORUS

CHORUS  
Split-screen duel at Forkgate,  
where every truth is partial light,  
where one voice names the structure  
and one voice names the bite.  

Split-screen duel at Forkgate,  
the gate is not a wall.  
It is the place the world reveals  
what each frame chose to call.  

And if the same event  
can wound in two directions,  
then the witness and the wound  
are both parts of the connection.

### CODA

RITSU  
Don’t call me the pattern.

WARDEN  
I won’t.

RITSU  
Don’t call me the exception.

WARDEN  
I won’t.

RITSU  
Then what do you call me?

WARDEN  
Unreduced.

CHORUS
