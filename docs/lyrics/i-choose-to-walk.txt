Title: I Choose to Walk / 歩くと決めた
ID: 25dcaa6a-c6c7-4302-bd29-f2f827957fd0
Prompt:
[Intro]
(whisper)
I choose to walk…
歩くと決めた。
Even if the ground remembers my name.

[Verse 1]
I wake up in the aftermath,
like the room is still apologizing.
My body keeps receipts in quiet places—
shoulders, jaw, the back of my eyes.

痛みは証拠みたいで
消えないインクのまま
「大丈夫」って言いながら
心はまだ 逃げ道を探す

I learned to smile in self-defense,
learned “fine” like a practiced lie.
But tonight I’m done pretending
that surviving is the same as living.

[Pre-Chorus]
If I’m a ship with a cracked hull,
I’m still a ship.
If I’m a map with burned-out edges,
I still point somewhere.

傷は終わりじゃない
地図の線になる
怖くても 揺れてても
ここにいる まだ

[Chorus]
I choose to walk—not because I’m fearless,
but because I’m tired of kneeling to the past.
I choose to walk with my hands still shaking,
with a heart that doesn’t trust “safe” yet.

歩くと決めた
涙の重さでも
不確かさを抱いて
それでも 進む

Let the improbable find me.
Let the unlikely be true.
Even if all I have is one small step—
I choose to walk.

[Verse 2]
Some days I flinch at sunlight,
like joy is a door that slams.
Some memories return like perfume,
and some return wearing smoke.

思い出が刺す夜に
「戻らない」って誓う
忘れるんじゃなくて
抱え方を変えていく

I don’t need a clean story,
I don’t need a perfect arc.
I just need one moment
where I don’t abandon myself.

[Pre-Chorus]
I used to measure love in damage,
mistook chaos for gravity.
But healing is a strange math—
it adds what I thought was gone.

傷跡は残る
でも そこに光が入る
折れたままの私が
立てる理由になる

[Chorus]
I choose to walk—not because I’m fearless,
but because I’m tired of kneeling to the past.
I choose to walk with my hands still shaking,
with a heart that doesn’t trust “safe” yet.

歩くと決めた
昨日の影を連れて
信じ切れなくても
それでも 進む

Let the improbable find me.
Let the unlikely be true.
Even if all I have is one small step—
I choose to walk.

[Bridge]
Maybe I never get closure.
Maybe the “why” stays open like a wound.
But I can stop feeding the fire
with my own breath.

ねえ 過去よ
私の人生を返して
奪った分だけ
未来で取り戻す

I don’t forgive what broke me—
I release the right it had to rule me.
I’m not healed like a miracle.
I’m healed like a practice.

ゆっくりでいい
震えてていい
「生きる」を選ぶたび
私は強くなる

[Final Chorus]
I choose to walk—not because I’m ready,
but because I’m here, and that counts.
I choose to walk through the static and maybe
toward a life I can finally pronounce.

歩くと決めた
名前のない希望でも
不可能に近いなら
なおさら 行く

Let the improbable find me.
Let the unlikely be true.
If my scars are the price of being real,
then I pay it—
and I choose to walk.

[Outro]
(soft)
歩くと決めた。
I choose to walk.
And if I fall—
I’ll stand up in my own time.
