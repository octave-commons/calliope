## STYLE PROMPT

**Genre Fusion:** Post-Industrial Pressure Music × Ritual Glitch Rock × Breath-Guided IDM × Grunge Noise-Ambient × Deconstructed Club Architecture × Somatic Monologue Pop

**Tuning / Tempo:** 432Hz, 88–100 BPM with micro-tempo instability (subtle drift ±2 BPM)

**Key Center:** C minor drifting toward F modal minor, frequent chromatic intrusion (Db, Gb, E natural for tension)

**Rhythmic Architecture:**

* Kick like distant machinery (low, rounded, chest-heavy)
* Snare as metal impact / warehouse reverb slap
* Asymmetrical breakcore fragments in transitions
* Drop sections with half-time collapse feel
* Silence pockets used as structural reset points

**Texture Palette:**

* Percussive detuned piano clusters (felt-hammer texture)
* Rusted reverb tails
* Air-pressure sub bass haze
* Industrial field recordings (vent hum, chain drag, concrete scrape)
* Choir pads stretched and granularized
* Neural flicker glitch bursts in upper frequencies
* Saturation bloom during emotional peaks

**Vocal Direction:**

* Close-mic breath ASMR verses (intimate, almost confessional)
* Layered whispered Japanese phrases under English lead lines
* Chorus expands into distorted choral stack (gender-neutral blend)
* Occasional spoken internal monologue lines with slight pitch drop

**Mood:** Sacred but mechanical. Vulnerable but resilient. Dark warehouse lit by violet sodium lamps. Emotional catharsis without sentimentality.
