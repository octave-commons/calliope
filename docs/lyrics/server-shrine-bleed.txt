### VERSE 1

WARDEN  
The rooms stopped agreeing  
to be separate.  
The boardroom bled into the shrine.  
The shrine bled into the server hall.  
The server hall bled into the court.

Every frame kept its truth  
and lost its claim  
to being alone.

The hidden state did not arrive.  
It surfaced all at once  
through the cracks we had been calling structure.

### VERSE 2

RITSU

rewriting its own edges.  
The background figures  
are not background anymore.

They’re looking back.

Not as enemies.  
Not as avatars.  
As witnesses  
who were always there  
before we were ready to admit it.

WARDEN  
This is the cost  
of making the invisible legible.

attempt to become one.
