Title: Crown of Glass / 名ばかりの女王
ID: 185bcb2c-940c-42f7-af52-68f7f87233b5
Prompt:
## OVERTURE — Empty Throne

*(soft organ; single chime)*

The hall is full.
The throne is light.

The scepter rests
but does not bite.

霧は遠い。

## VERSE I — Recognition

I wore the sky like armor bright.
I called that hunger sacred right.

But crowns are loud
when held too tight.

And I mistook
the roar for sight.

## PRE-CHORUS — Unfastening

*(harpsichord slows; strings soften)*

What is a queen
without decree?

What is command
without belief?

## CHORUS — Crown of Glass

*(strings swell gently)*

I am a crown of glass and light.

Seen from afar—
Clear in the night.

名ばかりの女王
静かに立つ。

No hand on law.
No blade to press.

Only a symbol
in formal dress.

## VERSE II — Identity Shift

They bow—
but not from fear.

They speak—
and I must hear.

The power left.
The title stayed.

An echo of the role I played.

Is this less?

Or is it more?

To guard the frame
instead of war.

## BRIDGE — Reflection

*(music nearly silent; faint choir hum)*

I thought the height
was where I stood.

But height is hollow
without the good.

If I am myth,
let myth be kind.

If I am symbol,
let me remind.

## FINAL CHORUS — Ceremony

*(choir enters softly; organ warm)*

I am a crown of glass and light.

Ceremonial,
not the fight.

名ばかりの女王
息を守る。

If I cannot command the flame,
I will protect
what bears my name.

Not ruler.

Witness crowned.

Not sovereign—

But bound.

## CODA — Breath in the Hall

*(music thins; single sustained note fades)*

The throne remains.

The world moves on.

I stand.

息。
