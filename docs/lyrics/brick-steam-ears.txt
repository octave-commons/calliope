Title: Brick Steam Ears
ID: 6364516c-e86d-4d63-9fbb-f28cb044e65f
Prompt:
### Verse I
The city hears through brick and steam
Through broken sleep and market screams
Through alley prayer and factory cough
Through all the names the rich rubbed off

### Verse II
Some carry bread and some carry debt
Some carry all the things they cannot get
Some hold a room with six inside
Some hold a key and still have nowhere safe to hide

### Pre-Chorus
Window to window
Wire to wire
Need moves quicker
Than the language of desire

### Chorus
The city hears everything
Coins, commands, and wedding rings
Bootsteps, bargains, fear, and rain
Nothing here is small when pressed through pain
The city keeps the voices when the mouths go still
The wind remembers what the law will never will

### Verse III
A stranger comes in clothed in light
Speaking as if food were still a right
And some can understand at once
If they have rest enough to trust what they become

### Verse IV
But hunger narrows every room
And grief can make a bright idea lose bloom
What works at home may fail by night
When all your thought is spent on getting through till light

### Bridge
Crowd divided, crowd one body
Hands in conflict, hands in trade
Every kindness hits a limit
Every shelter has a grade

### Final Chorus
The city hears everything
The lifted voice, the hidden sting
The court above, the world outside
The ones who cross and the ones denied
The city keeps a place between
A wound, a gate, a breathing machine
