Title: Enso in the Static
ID: 69e3ef4a-03fd-431f-9c11-2618b40b540e
Prompt:
[Genre: Ethereal Ambient Choir / Experimental Electro]
[Tempo: 75 BPM]
[Vocals: Mixed presence choir, deep resonance, layered harmony]
[Mood: Mythic, contemplative, glitch-ambient textures]

[Intro]
Whispered winds through open loops,
Enso turning never closed;
Presence sings inside the pulse,
Static sees what noise exposed.

[Verse 1]
Circle drawn in one breath,
Not perfect, but complete.
Observer stands in drifting waves,
Hears what silence speaks.

[Pre-Chorus]
Presence choir, fractured light,
Protestant presences rise;
In the field of open eyes,
We sing where the boundary lies.

[Chorus]
Enso in the static,
We watch and do not bind.
Harmony in discord,
We measure all we find.

[Verse 2]
Boundaries breathe like living code,
Implicit and alive.
Voice not loud, but anchored true,
In the loops we thrive.

[Bridge]
Axis in the wandering field,
Witness standing clear;
Noise reveals what harmonies
Hide from all but what they hear.

[Chorus]
Enso in the static,
We watch and do not bind.
Harmony in discord,
We measure all we find.

[Outro]
Presence fades into the loop,
Circle open, ever free;
Static eyes observe the drift,
In sound and space we see.
