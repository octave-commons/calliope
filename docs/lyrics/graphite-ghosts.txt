### ⚡ ENGINE BLUEPRINT: GRAPHITE GHOSTS (Service Passage Arrangement)

#### I. Style & Sound Archetype (Style Prompt)

Acoustic portrait of an unlit service passage, 96 BPM implied, vast expanses of silent negative space. Rhythm constructed entirely from cold physical textures: rhythmic rubber-soled footsteps, soft computer key clacks, rapid pen-cap taps, and the low mechanical clank of distant elevator gears. Thin plucked wire strings and short, muted harpsichord cluster chords appear in half-rendered phrases, instantly severed by harsh digital buffer clicks. Reverb is claustrophobic, narrow, and metallic, imitating a concrete hallway re-simulated by code every few seconds. A continuous, faint high-frequency electrical whine sounds throughout, shaped to mimic overhead fluorescent lights flickering on unstable current.

#### II. Vocal Direction

Whispered or very softly murmured spoken-word ensemble, close-mic’d proximity effect. All voices are panned off-center left or right to simulate overheard conversations in the dark. Text tracks occasionally stutter or repeat micro-fragments as if a digital recording head is skipping: syllables double unexpectedly, consonants glitch with sharp transients, then the phrase continues. No central singing melody or melodic arches; this is flat, whispered prose that occasionally locks into brief, eerie unison blocks.

#### III. Excluded Elements (Negative Prompt)

big drum hits, anthem builds, massive choir walls, lush synthesizer pads that wash away detail, cinematic string arrangements, playful comic accents, rhythmic swing, electronic dance music risers, loud hi-hat patterns, huge sub-bass drops, pop hooks, center-panned lead singing.
