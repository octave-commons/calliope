# calliope — World Bible (Entities & Lore)

*Named-entity extraction over 690 songs. Status: derived/provisional —
glosses are interpretive; quoted lines are observed. Contradictions are
kept, not resolved (epiphany rule: similarity ≠ identity).*

## The laws of the world

1. **Fork Tax** — branching the world is permitted but taxed; payment is
   proof, not money. "Not guilt—just interest on an unproven branch."
   "Fork Tax compounds till the branch can't move."
2. **Gate law** — gates open only for reproducible proof: "The Gate don't
   care how clean your metaphors look—it only opens for what survives the
   hook." "The gates don't separate worlds. They separate explanations."
3. **Drift rule** — "if μ can't match a golden record, it's drift—so it's η
   until proven otherwise." Hysteresis is law: two clean receipts to climb
   back from a demotion.
4. **Memory law** — nothing is deleted; "additive until limit, then
   consolidate." "Less remembered isn't lost—it's staged." "Never remove
   constraints; disable instead."
5. **Time law** — "no 'today,' say the date, say the zone; America/Phoenix
   carved into stone."
6. **Stewardship** — "Promotion to μ requires a RoleSignature hash—truth has
   a steward. You can't crown it anonymously."
7. **Echo law** — "if it doesn't survive two echoes, it doesn't get to call
   itself μ." The Echo Choir refuses to sing μ on the first pass.
8. **Toxin handling** — "η is only Π if it contains signal; toxin labels are
   dropped on receipt."
9. **Entropy bounds** — presence exists only when H_min < H < H_max.
10. **The Event already happened** — "the world is not healed. It is
    witnessed." The fantasy game was dual-use labor harvesting: "The game
    didn't crash. The real world did."
