Title: Π := **Synthesis → Plan → Program**

Formally:

* Inputs: (N) (normalized signals), constraints (C), goal (G)
* Output: a *candidate policy/plan* (\pi) (sequence of actions + decision rules)

[
\Pi(N, C, G) \to \pi
]

Where a plan/policy can be modeled as:

[
\pi := \langle \text{Steps }(a_1,\dots,a_k),\ \text{StopConditions},\ \text{Fallbacks},\ \text{SuccessMetrics}\rangle
]

In your loop:

[
P \rightarrow R \rightarrow N \rightarrow \Pi \rightarrow A \rightarrow (feedback) \rightarrow P
]

Π is the moment raw/cleaned understanding becomes *an executable intent* (what to do, in what order, under what guardrails).
