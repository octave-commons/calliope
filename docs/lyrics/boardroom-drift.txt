Title: **Boardroom Drift**
ID: 6b1c1124-08a9-4b2e-93f5-14bdb1bfc17e
Prompt:
*(A room with no visible doors. One light. A hum below the floor. A single harpsichord note repeats and fails to resolve.)*

### INTRO

WARDEN  
I have the board.  
I have the feed.  
I have the version each room sees.

But none of them overlap cleanly.  
None of them arrive complete.  
Every frame is true  
and none is the truth.

### VERSE 1

WARDEN  
In the court, they call it drift.  
In the boardroom, they call it noise.  
In the Gate, they call it lag  
when the world starts choosing its voice.

I can read the thresholds.  
I can map the pressure shifts.  
I can name the pattern  
as it crosses and re-fits.

But naming is not collapse.  
Naming is not cure.  
And if I force the whole into one  
I may destroy what was still real.

### RITSU — FRAGMENTS

RITSU  
...there’s someone in the—

WARDEN  
What?

RITSU  
...in the next room.  
No, not a room.  
A place that feels like one.  
It keeps changing when I—

### VERSE 2

WARDEN  
There are seven versions of the same event  
and none are lying.  
That is the problem.

The room with the throne says authority.  
The room with the screens says metrics.  
The room with the avatars says play.  
The room with the waiting hands says prayer.  

I stand in the seam  
where all of them touch  
and none of them agree  
on what the seam is for.

### RITSU — FRAGMENTS

RITSU  
...I can hear it breathing.  
...the background people—  
...they look at me like—

WARDEN  
Like what?

RITSU  
...like I matter  
more than I should.

### PRE-CHORUS

WARDEN  
If I call it one story,  
I lie to the others.  
If I call it many stories,  
I abandon the bridge.

If I publish the whole shape,  
someone will use it to flatten the living edge.  
If I stay silent,  
the wrong hands will name it first.

### CHORUS

LOW CHORUS  
No one sees the whole Gate.  
No one owns the total light.  
Every witness gets a fraction  
and mistakes it for the right.

No one sees the whole Gate.  
Every log is partial truth.  
What is saved is only saved  
by what it refuses to reduce.

### BRIDGE

*(The harpsichord stops. A sub-bass swell, then near silence.)*

WARDEN  
I can see the cluster.  
I can see the pressure.  
I can see the pattern leaning  
toward a shape it will not yet say.

But the shape is held together  
by what the people inside still are.  
If I name it too cleanly,  
I may break the thing I’m trying to guard.

RITSU  
...don’t make me smaller.  
...just because you can see me.

WARDEN  
I won’t.

RITSU  
...promise?

WARDEN  
That’s not my role.

### FINAL CHORUS

LOW CHORUS  
No one sees the whole Gate,  
but the Gate keeps opening.  
No one owns the total truth,  
but the truth keeps moving.

No one sees the whole Gate.  
The witness pays in fracture.  
The world survives the partial view  
until the hidden state grows larger.

### CODA

*(One held organ tone. A distant chime, as if a system accepted a packet and did not understand it.)*

WARDEN  
I have the board.  
I have the feed.  
I have the burden.

And still I cannot say  
what it all is  
without changing it.

RITSU  
...then don’t.
