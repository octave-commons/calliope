Title: Glass Table Names
ID: ae22d9e2-8f2d-4637-a4d9-ef97b5577654
Prompt:

*(A room with daylight again. Not the same room. A table. Several chairs. Nothing ceremonial yet — which is why it can become ceremonial later.)*

### INTRO

RIN  
We keep what works.  
We name what broke.  
We do not pretend  
those are the same thing.

WARDEN  
No.

RITSU  
And we share the names.

### VERSE 1

WARDEN  
I thought a commons  
was a place.  
It is not.

It is a decision  
to refuse private ownership  
of the fragile parts.

It is a promise  
that the people most affected  
get a voice before the system  
calls itself complete.

RITSU  
That’s new.

WARDEN  
No.

RITSU  
It is.  
Because before,  
the room spoke first  
and called it order.

### VERSE 2

RIN  
Now the room listens.  
Or it doesn’t get to claim  
it is the room.

The detector stays open.  
The logs stay readable.  
The criteria stay public.  
The harm stays nameable  
without requiring a priesthood.

WARDEN  
And when the signal is unclear?

RIN  
We mark it unclear.  
We don’t force certainty  
to make the dashboard prettier.

RITSU  
That sounds almost gentle.

RIN  
It is.  
And it is not weak.

### PRE-CHORUS

CHORUS  
Residual light is still light.  
Residual light is enough  
to see where the floor has changed.  
Residual light is enough  
to keep another fall from looking like fate.

### BRIDGE

*(The choir is small, but close. One or two voices at a time, like people speaking around a shared table.)*

WARDEN  
We could build this badly.

RIN  
We could.

WARDEN  
We could turn vigilance  
into another gate.

RITSU  
Then don’t.

WARDEN  
That’s not simple.

RITSU  
No.  
It’s just the work.

### FINAL CHORUS

ALL  
The commons of residual light,  
small fire, shared breath,  
where nothing is redeemed by magic  
and nothing is erased by depth.  

The commons of residual light,  
we keep the broken true.  
We build a room that knows its wounds  
and still knows what to do.  

The commons of residual light,  
not victory, not end.  
A place where surviving together  
becomes a way to mend.

### CODA

*(A few seconds of near silence. Then the sound of chairs moving, not in panic, but in use.)*

WARDEN  
Who decides?

RITSU  
We do.

RIN  
Together.

CHORUS  
:: commons established ::  
:: provisional ::  
:: durable ::
