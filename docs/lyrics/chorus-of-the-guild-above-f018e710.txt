Title: Chorus of the Guild Above / 天上ギルドの合唱
ID: a450cd87-5c96-45a7-8f55-bd364563b25c
Tags: 
**Genre/Style:** Fast baroque-pop opera × gothic choral spectacle × MMO mythic commentary. 126 BPM driving 4/4. Rapid harpsichord ostinatos, racing strings, cathedral organ swells, militant snare, rolling timpani. Large mixed choir (SATB) with call-and-response passages. Subtle glitch shimmer under transitions.

**Vocal Direction:** Primarily choral narration (Greek chorus energy). Alternating male and female leads. Occasional soaring soprano descants above the choir. Crisp diction, percussive consonants, dramatic dynamic shifts.

**Mood:** Regal observation. Ironic reverence. Awe without allegiance.

**Structure:** Orchestral Overture → Chorus Verse I (narration) → Pre-Chorus (rising tension) → Grand Refrain → Chorus Verse II → Bridge (whispered guild counsel) → Final Refrain (full blaze) → Echoing coda.


--- Prompt ---
## OVERTURE — Balcony of Light

*(organ swell; choir hum; strings racing beneath)*

From the ramparts of reflected sky,
We watch the velvet falcon fly.

And on the marble, unbowed flame,
Stands the one who will not claim.

天上より見守る。

## CHORUS VERSE I — Witness

Sopranos:
Crown of frost and silver decree—

Tenors:
Spine of steel in defiant key—

Altos:
Blade of song in satin air—

Basses:
Grounded pulse that does not care—

All:
Thus begins the sacred duel,
Pride as scepter, breath as rule.

## PRE-CHORUS — Divided Air

*(strings ascending; timpani rumble)*

High above the shattering nave,
No one kneels and none are saved.

Tick—tock—
threads align.

Is this fate?
Or design?

## GRAND REFRAIN — We Bear No Banner

*(full orchestral surge; choir in harmony)*

We bear no banner—
We cast no stone.

We witness fire
against the throne.

鷹の女王
跪かない

Two bright notes in mirrored sky,
Neither willing yet to die.

We bear no banner—
We take no side.

In spectacle,
we do not hide.

## CHORUS VERSE II — The Measure

Sopranos:
Hear the aria cut like glass—

Tenors:
Hear the answer—steady, brass—

Altos:
Velocity and vertical flame—

Basses:
Continuance without a name—

All:
What is power without a crown?
What is rising without down?

## BRIDGE — Guild Counsel

*(music drops; whispering choir; faint glitch shimmer)*

Observe.

Do not inherit the rage.

Remember:
Every queen was once a page.

Remember:
Every rebel risks a throne.

Remember:
Breath is never owned.

息は借り物。

## FINAL REFRAIN — Vast Arena

*(orchestra blazing; choir at full force; soprano descant above)*

We bear no banner—
but we ascend
In understanding
without end.

Crown and spine,
blade and ground—

Both are bound
by common sound.

天上ギルド
静かに歌う。

We bear no banner—
we remain.

Witness to glory.
Witness to flame.

## CODA — Echo in the Heights

*(choir fades; organ softens)*

In every duel of frost and will,
The sky is watching—

Still.

息。
