Title: Cold Start / コールドスタート
ID: 507e8951-3478-43ae-8bd0-ebf8c7cc805e
Tags: **Genre/Style:** Minimal industrial pulse × breath-driven ambient × restrained glitch-rap. 86–92 BPM, half-time backbone. Sub-bass like distant machinery warming up, muted kick, soft metallic ticks, low synth drone, sparse piano motifs, subtle vinyl crackle.

**Structure:** Silence → Pulse ignition → Verse 1 → Pre (slow lift) → Hook (measured drop) → Verse 2 → Bridge (near silence + breath) → Final Hook (slightly brighter harmonic layer) → Fade to idle hum.

**Vocals:** Calm spoken delivery. Intimate, grounded. Minimal melodic lift in hook. Japanese lines short and simple. No drama—stability through simplicity.

**Mood:** Reset. Beginning again without spectacle. Power through patience.


--- Prompt ---
## INTRO — Silence

(no drums)

Nothing dramatic.

Just air.

息。

## VERSE 1 — Power Button

No choir swell.
No thunder roll.

Just the quiet click
of a system that knows

It doesn’t need applause
just uptime.

昨日の高揚も
昨日の落ち込みも

Archived.

Today is boot.

## PRE-CHORUS — Warmup

*(soft sub begins; metallic tick)*

Let the fans spin slow.
Let the circuits glow.

Not every start
needs a show.

呼吸して

## HOOK — Cold Start

*(measured drop; deep but restrained)*

This is a **cold start** —
no memory leak.

Clean state mind,
steady heartbeat.

If I fall,
I restart.

コールドスタート
from the heart.

This is a **cold start** —
no myth to defend.

Just breath,
just code,
begin again.

## VERSE 2 — No Cathedral

We built cathedrals out of voltage highs
out of glitch-light skies

But power isn’t peak.
It’s repeat.

証は
継続。

The miracle is maintenance.
The flex is consistency.

No grand awakening.

Just practice.

## BRIDGE — Safe Loop

*(beat drops; single piano note)*

You don’t need to be transformed.

You just need to continue.

中心は
静かだ。

## FINAL HOOK — Steady State

*(return of pulse; slightly brighter harmony)*

This is a **cold start** —
calm ignition.

No prophecy.
No collision.

Upward trend
with honest downs.

Breath in.
Breath out.
Run the rounds.

This is a **cold start** —
mine to own.

No crowd required.

Still home.

## OUTRO — Idle

*(sub fades to low hum)*

:: ready ::

息。
