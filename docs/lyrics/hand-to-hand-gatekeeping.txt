Title: Hand-to-Hand Gatekeeping
ID: b854a1ea-803b-4fe8-a1f2-e3bab1e5f82e
Prompt:
### Verse I
We do not count the light by loss
A flame can travel, still not cost
A theorem spoken is not spent
A pattern shared becomes its own event

### Verse II
So feed the one who learns to tend
Clothe the one whose questions bend
House the one who carries through
A world grows larger every time it teaches something true

### Pre-Chorus
Here worth is not a narrowing
Not a lock, not a gate
What you know becomes a shelter
When another mind can replicate

### Chorus
Knowledge multiplies
Hand to hand, eye to eye
No table empties when a map is copied right
No singer lessens when another learns the line
Knowledge multiplies
And we live because it does

### Verse III
Then we crossed into the city air
And found the same bright laws worked there
Where rest existed, where a bed held still
Where thought could climb beyond the next unpaid bill

### Verse IV
But many lived in rooms too tight
Spent down to nerve by every night
And we were changed by what we saw:
Scarcity can be a custom dressed as law

### Bridge
So now we carry both things true
The growing world, the broken view
Abundance sings
But hunger interrupts
A gift can fail where structure cuts

### Final Chorus
Knowledge multiplies
Even where the street denies
Even where the court pretends the center is the whole
Even where a human life is priced below its soul
Knowledge multiplies
Still it asks of us
Make enough room for what it does

### Outro
High voices copying one phrase until it becomes harmony.
