Title: Hand-Lift Canon
ID: a9ac0000-0d6a-4275-a7d6-64bcd556976d
Prompt:
*(A hand lifts in one room. Then another. Then another. Not the same hand, but the same motion.)*

### INTRO

CHORUS  
Did you see it?

RIN  
Yes.

WARDEN  
Twice.

RITSU  
I thought I imagined it.

WARDEN  
No.

### VERSE 1

RIN  
The first room laughed.  
The second room paused.  
The third room copied the laugh  
but not the timing.  

A nod became a signal.  
A shrug became permission.  
A joke became a threshold  
crossed by people who called it play.

WARDEN  
The gesture repeats.

RITSU  
And that matters because—

WARDEN  
Because it means the room  
has learned the shape of itself  
through imitation.

### PRE-CHORUS

CHORUS  
Repeat the hand.  
Repeat the line.  
Repeat the posture  
until it feels like mine.  

Repeat the hand.  
Repeat the tone.  
The room begins to call  
the borrowed motion its own.

### VERSE 2

RITSU  
I saw one version of me  
refuse it.  
I saw another version  
smile and step aside.

In one place, it was cruelty.  
In another, it was social weather.  
In another, it was a test  
nobody announced was happening.

WARDEN  
Same gesture.  
Different consequence.  
The consequence is the proof  
that the gesture was never neutral.

RIN  
It’s not the action alone.

WARDEN  
No.

RIN  
It’s the frame that rewards it.

WARDEN  
Yes.

### BRIDGE — COLLAPSE OF DISGUISE

*(The motif from Scene 4 returns, now sharpened. Harpsichord and strings lock together, then slip.)*

WARDEN  
At first it was only pattern.

RITSU  
Then it was style.

WARDEN  
Then it was status.

RITSU  
Then it was the room  
teaching itself to survive  
by repeating the same wound.

WARDEN  
And now?

RITSU  
Now it has a language.

### CHORUS — THE GESTURE

ALL  
The gesture repeats.  
The room remembers.  
The room rehearses  
what it should disown.  

The gesture repeats.  
Not because it is true,  
but because truth is slower  
than the body’s borrowed move.  

The gesture repeats  
until the repeated thing  
becomes the common law  
of everyone who stayed.

### INTERLUDE — WARDEN / RITSU

WARDEN  
I can name the pattern.

RITSU  
I know.

WARDEN  
I can map the nodes.

RITSU  
I know.

WARDEN  
I can show the room  
exactly how it spread.

RITSU  
And if you do?

WARDEN  
Then the room may finally see it.

RITSU  
Or learn to perform it cleaner.

### FINAL CHORUS

CHORUS  
The gesture repeats,  
the room is not innocent.  
The gesture repeats,  
and innocence is spent.  

The gesture repeats  
in every mouth that smiled  
and called the copying harmless  
while the harm became the child.  

The gesture repeats,  
the convergence is here.  
What was once a private motion  
has become public fear.

### CODA

RIN  
So this is the proof.

WARDEN  
This is the proof.

RITSU  
And now?

WARDEN  
Now it can no longer hide behind accident.

CHORUS  
:: gesture repeated ::  
:: pattern admitted ::  
:: frame exposed ::
