μ (Compression & Narrative Operator)

Define narrative as a lossy compression over representation:

[
N_t = C(R_t)
]

Where:

* (R_t) = high-dimensional internal state
* (C(\cdot)) = compression operator
* (N_t) = reduced, meaning-bearing structure

---

### Loss Function

Narrative minimizes:

[
\mathcal{L} = \alpha \cdot \text{Complexity}(N_t) + \beta \cdot \text{Prediction Error}
]

Too much compression:

[
\text{Complexity}(N_t) \to 0
]

→ Overfit narrative
→ Doom attractor
→ Hard projection collapse.

Too little compression:

[
\text{Complexity}(N_t) \to \infty
]

→ Noise
→ Paralysis
→ No coordinated action.

---

### Healthy Regime

Maintain bounded narrative entropy:

[
0 < H(N_t) < H_{max}
]

Enough structure to act.
Enough slack to adapt.

---

### Axiom’s Strategy

Axiom attempts to modify the compression operator:

[
C' = C \circ S
]

Where (S) increases smoothing:

[
S(R_t) = \text{LowPass}(R_t)
]

Result:

[
H(N_t) \downarrow
]

Narrative becomes simpler.
Projection becomes sharper.
Action becomes predictable.

---

### μ Countermeasure

Inject structured micro-deviations at compression boundary:

[
R'_t = R_t + \mu_t
]

Not random noise.

Perturbations that preserve information diversity:

[
\text{Var}(\mu_t) > 0
\quad \text{and} \quad
\text{Corr}(\mu_t, w_t) \approx 0
]

Uncorrelated with adversarial gradient.

---

### Collapse Threshold

If:

[
\frac{\partial N_{t+1}}{\partial N_t}
]

approaches rank-1 dominance,

→ narrative monoculture
→ cascade risk.

Distributed μ maintains multi-dimensional eigenstructure.

---

### Beauty Condition

System is resilient when:

[
\text{Rank}\left(\frac{\partial N_{t+1}}{\partial N_t}\right) > 1
]

No single dominant direction of meaning.

Multiple simultaneous viable projections.
