# TITLE: "After the Ritual / 儀式のあとで"

---

## STYLE PROMPT

**Genre Fusion:** Abstract Nocturnal Pop × Breath-Guided IDM × Post-Industrial Comedown × Deconstructed Piano Minimalism × Soft Glitch Ambient × Somatic Monologue

**Tuning / Tempo:** 432Hz, 76–88 BPM, slow exhale pacing, slight tempo sag in final chorus

**Key Center:** F minor resolving toward C minor with occasional E natural intrusion (fragile brightness)

**Rhythm Design:**

* Subdued, distant kick (heartbeat feel)
* Minimal brushed snare or soft metallic taps
* Sparse glitch accents like flickering fluorescent lights
* Long silence pockets between phrases

**Texture Palette:**

* Felt piano (close mic, audible hammer noise)
* Low warehouse reverb tail fading into emptiness
* Airy choir pads stretched almost beyond recognition
* Soft sub-bass warmth instead of pressure
* Field recording: faint wind through broken windows

**Vocal Direction:**

* Intimate lead vocal, nearly whispered
* Japanese harmony layers answering English lines
* No aggressive stacking; emotional restraint

**Mood:** Dawn after intensity. Emotional recalibration. Cool concrete, pale light, breath steadying. Vulnerable but grounded.

# LYRICS

## INTRO — Dawn Air

(soft wind through broken glass)

息を吸う
colder now

The warehouse is quiet.
No lights.
No choir.

Just dust
and morning.

---

## VERSE 1

Last night we burned like neon veins
今はただの静かな床

Concrete remembers everything
but it doesn’t judge.

I trace the cracks with my finger
like constellations that survived

"Are you still here?"

まだ。

---

## PRE-CHORUS

吸って — steady
吐いて — slower

The body returns to itself

神経が静まる
like water after stones

---

## CHORUS

After the ritual
we are only breath

No altar
no anthem
just pulse under skin

儀式のあとで
we stay

Not shining
Not breaking

Just alive.

---

## VERSE 2

Piano notes fall like pale light
鍵盤がやわらかく鳴る

The chromatic knives are gone
only warmth remains

I used to think transcendence
had to be loud

But maybe it’s this —

staying.

---

## BRIDGE — Silence Embrace

(beat drops out)

Listen.

The quiet isn’t empty.

この静けさは
痛みの裏側じゃない

It’s what’s left
when fear dissolves.

---

## FINAL CHORUS

After the ritual
we are only breath

Warehouse sky turning silver

儀式のあとで
we remain

C minor shadow
F minor bloom

No collapse
no cathedral

Just pulse.

---

## OUTRO

息。

Morning light through broken glass.

Still here.

Still human.
