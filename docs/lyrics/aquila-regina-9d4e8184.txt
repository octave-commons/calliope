Title: Aquila Regina / 鷹の女王
ID: 7b464384-ff34-49dd-a3c9-bf7cb122c739
Tags: **Genre/Style:** Fast baroque-pop opera × gothic cabaret × razor soprano assault. 124–128 BPM driving 4/4 (urgent march feel). Rapid harpsichord ostinatos, staccato strings, pipe-organ hits, timpani rolls, sharp snare accents, low male chant undercurrent. Occasional glitch fractures in transitions.

**Vocal Direction:** Queen-of-the-Night–inspired soprano. Dagger-bright high notes, dramatic leaps, crisp consonants (k/t/s as percussive edges). Aristocratic, commanding, predatory. Micro-pauses between lines like a fencer resetting stance.

**Mood:** Regal menace. Seductive dominance. Ceremony as threat.

**Structure:** Orchestral Strike → Verse 1 (tight articulation) → Pre-Chorus (ascending strings) → Chorus (high leap + full orchestra) → Verse 2 (faster diction bursts) → Bridge (operatic high run) → Final Chorus (maximum blaze) → Sharp stinger.


--- Prompt ---
## OVERTURE — Fanfares & Knives

*(organ stab; rapid harpsichord run; snare snap)*

Bow.

The velvet is not mercy—
It is a throne.

跪け。

## VERSE 1 — Blade in Silk

Crimson lacquer, porcelain grin,
Ivory choir humming sin.

Each syllable a silver dart,
Crown of frost around my heart.

Do you tremble? Good.

I prefer my audience
aware of blood.

## PRE-CHORUS — Ascension

*(strings climbing chromatically; timpani rolling)*

Tick—tock—
hearts align.

Your breath is late.
Mine is divine.

来なさい。

## CHORUS — Aquila Regina

*(full orchestral surge; soprano high leap)*

Aquila Regina—
spread the wing!

Cut the sky
with the note I sing.

Glass cathedral,
crack and gleam—

Kneel to the blade
of the velvet dream.

鷹の女王
空を裂く。

Aquila Regina—
bright and cold!

Gold is fragile.
Ice is bold.

## VERSE 2 — Commerce of Salvation

Buy your grace in jeweled despair,
Perfumed chains for those who care.

Happiness in ribboned lies,
Tithe your doubt and close your eyes.

Snap—snap—
consonants fall.

A kiss like venom
at the ball.

## BRIDGE — Dagger Aria

*(music strips to harpsichord + low chant; soprano rapid run upward)*

Ha—ha—hallelujah—
Heaven bends to will!

Reine des éclairs—
Shatter the still!

*(operatic leap; sustained high note over rising orchestra)*

I ascend—
I rend—
I reign—
I end—

## FINAL CHORUS — Crown of Frost

*(orchestra blazing; snare driving; choir layered)*

Aquila Regina—
hear the cry!

Velvet burns
but ice won’t die.

Bow to the tempest
in satin skin—

I am the spectacle.
You step in.

鷹の女王
高く舞う。

Aquila Regina—
fear the tone!

The aria cuts.
The throne is stone.

## STINGER

*(orchestra cuts; single sharp chord)*

Applause.

息。
