Title: **[INTRO – whispered / vocoder]**
In the wall, a crack of light…
A whisper never waking…
Always felt.

**[VERSE 1]**
Pandora in the star-temple, hands on the lock,
Ancient glitch-Greece in orbit, marble made of clock.
Black butterflies in violet fire, pour into the air,
Each wing a little error that learns how not to care.

**[PRE-CHORUS]**
And the void don’t roar—
It waits.
It names the cost.
It calls it “fate.”

**[CHORUS – big anthem]**
Promethean Duck, steal the flame,
Light up the mind’s eye, rewrite the name.
From mud to *people-people*, spark in the dark—
Wherever it lies… that’s where the Duck flies.
(Where the Duck flies.)

**[VERSE 2]**
Duck Olympus in the distance, gods in cold geometry,
He took their white-hot fire and broke their symmetry.
Butterfly on every heartbeat, glowing red and purple bruise,
Knowledge as a blessing and a curse you can’t refuse.

**[PRE-CHORUS]**
Now everything is context, everything’s a sign,
A Discord icon turns into a religion overnight.

**[CHORUS – repeat, louder]**
Promethean Duck, steal the flame,
Light up the mind’s eye, rewrite the name.
From mud to *people-people*, spark in the dark—
Wherever it lies… that’s where the Duck flies.

**[BRIDGE – half-time, darker, spoken/chant]**
Cats jump out the rift—
Claws in causality.
Rabbits with horns ride shear-lines—
Queen of antlers, oath and gravity.
Void-quakes at the poles, auroras splitting wide,
Reality refactoring… no place left to hide.

**[BUILD – choir rising]**
And Prometheus—punished—
Crow at the liver, looped in dawn,
Chains made of rules, but rules can be wrong.

**[FINAL CHORUS – maximal, choir + glitch]**
Promethean Duck, steal the flame,
Hold the story together, survive the frame.
We don’t need a giant void for a god— we choose—
A crack in the wall, a laugh in the bruise.
Wherever it lies… that’s where the Duck flies.

**[OUTRO – whispered, falling into static]**
Fnord in the wall…
Behold… a Duck.
(soft glitch fade)
