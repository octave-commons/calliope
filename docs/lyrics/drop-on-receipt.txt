Title: Drop on Receipt / 受信で破棄

Tags: **Genre/Style:** Dark cinematic bass × industrial sci‑fi × soft glitch ambient comedown. 92–100 BPM, half‑time crush. Massive sub drops, distorted bass hits, seismic impacts, metallic shrieks, warped orchestral stabs → then a drained, breath‑guided aftermath (felt piano, thin choir haze, warehouse reverb, wind-through-glass field recording).

**Structure:**

1. *Tension* (radio hiss + distant kick + breath) →
2. *Impact* (sub drop + orchestral stab) →
3. *Cascading collapse* (granular noise + stutters + broken transmission) →
4. *Echoing ruin* (piano minimalism + warm sub + long silence pockets).

**Vocals:** Minimal. Intimate near‑whisper lead with occasional distant screams stretched into texture. Reversed chants as pads. Japanese response-lines to English (call/answer). A few spoken “protocol lines” like a system readout.

**Mood:** Catastrophic clarity → boundary → survival warmth. Cosmic dread that resolves into steady breath.
