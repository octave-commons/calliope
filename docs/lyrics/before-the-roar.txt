Title: Before the Roar / 咆哮の前
ID: c0fca769-1e05-48de-a0c9-63fc1cdfe6ad
Prompt:
## OVERTURE — Cracked Sanctuary

*(low organ drone; distant water; falling debris echo)*

The cliffs are scarred.
The bridges sway.

Mist drifts through fractured stone.

霧はまだ落ちる。

## VERSE I — The Rebel (Mezzo)

I wanted justice—
clean and bright.

A blade to cut
through velvet night.

But look—

The pillars split.
The water choked.
The sacred arches
nearly broke.

This was not the world I meant.

証を求めた。

I summoned storm
and called it righteous—

Now I stand
in wreckage.

## INTERLUDE — The Guild (Choir, hushed)

Observe.
Measure.
Preserve.

System above spectacle.
Continuity above pride.

天上ギルド
静かに議す。

## VERSE II — The Queen (Soprano, growing intensity)

They watched me rise—
and called it threat.

They envied flame
and now regret.

Is it my fault
that I burn bright?

Is it my sin
that I claim height?

The world bends small
beneath my gaze.

If stone has cracked,
it’s stone that strays.

*(tone sharpens)*

I was ordained
by force of will—

If heaven trembles,
I reign still.

## ENSEMBLE — Under the Mist

*(all voices overlapping softly, never fully colliding)*

Rebel (low):
I did not mean for this to fall—

Guild (measured):
Stability must govern all—

Queen (ascending):
They fear the crown that I install—

Water (ambient swell):
…

## FINAL CADENCE — Edge of Decision

*(strings tremble; organ low; waterfall slightly louder)*

Three paths in fractured air.

One system trembling.

One will rising.

One conscience breaking.

咆哮の前。

息。
