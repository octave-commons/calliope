Title: Standardize Transparence
ID: 0021375c-588b-4589-9f56-7cecd1c19bf9
Prompt:
*(The table is still there. But now there are more papers on it. Some are requests. Some are demands disguised as requests.)*

### INTRO

RIN  
They noticed.

WARDEN  
Of course they did.

RITSU  
That’s fast.

RIN  
That’s what pressure does.  
It finds the shape that holds.

### VERSE 1

WARDEN  
The first pressure came polite.  
A request for access.  
A request for audit.  
A request to “standardize”  
what we had kept transparent.

The second pressure came smiling.  
A promise of resources.  
A promise of scale.  
A promise that the commons  
would be “more effective”  
if someone else held the keys.

RITSU  
That sounds like capture.

WARDEN  
It is capture  
when they rename the door  
and call it help.

### VERSE 2

RIN  
We built this so no one actor  
could decide what harm meant  
without being seen.

Now they want a chair  
at the table that decides  
what can be seen.

WARDEN  
And if we refuse?

RIN  
Then they call us obstructive.  
Then they call us emotional.  
Then they call the process  
more important than the people  
the process was made to protect.

RITSU  
I know that one.

### PRE-CHORUS

CHORUS  
Pressure on the commons,  
the old shape returns.  
Pressure on the commons,  
where every threshold burns.  

They do not need to break it.  
They only need to make it  
cost more to keep it open  
than to surrender what it names.

### BRIDGE

*(A low organ tone. Papers turning. A chair pulled back, then not used.)*

WARDEN  
We can’t panic.

RIN  
No.

WARDEN  
We can’t hide either.

RITSU  
No.

WARDEN  
So we make the rules visible again.

RIN  
And the values.

RITSU  
And the people behind them.

WARDEN  
That’s the part they hate.

RITSU  
Good.

### FINAL CHORUS

ALL  
Pressure on the commons,  
but the commons is not soft.  
It is held by mutual witness,  
not by permission from above.  

Pressure on the commons,  
and still we name the line:  
the tool is not for ownership.  
The truth is not a mine.  

Pressure on the commons,  
we do not give the frame away.  
We keep the doors transparent  
and we keep the gate in play.

### CODA

RIN  
They’ll come back.

WARDEN  
Yes.

RITSU  
And?

WARDEN  
And we’ll still be here  
if we do this right.

:: pressure logged ::  
:: commons retained ::
