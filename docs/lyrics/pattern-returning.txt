Title: Pattern Returning
ID: 130b94e8-99ba-43c3-a906-07c2b458334d
Prompt:
*(A motif begins. It is simple enough to remember, but not simple enough to trust.)*

### INTRO

CHORUS
One room said this.
Another room said that.
One room laughed.
Another room stalled.

The same motion,
different names.
The same pressure,
different halls.

RIN
Listen.

### VERSE 1

RIN
The first sign was not a scream.
It was a pattern returning
with its edges slightly changed.

A phrase I had never met before
appeared in three mouths at once.
A joke, a warning, a signal,
all wearing the same skin.

I asked the room what it meant.
The room answered in procedure.
I asked the Gate what it wanted.
The Gate repeated the gesture.

### RITORNELLO

ALL
What returns is not the same.
What changes is not lost.
We keep calling it coincidence
until the cost becomes a law.

What returns is not the same.
Still it comes back to us.
The unshared world is learning
how to sound like one.

### VERSE 2

WARDEN
I marked the recurrence.
I marked the drift.
I marked the way the same shape
crossed different thresholds.

One account.
Then three.
Then seven.

Not copy.
Not echo.
Not error.

A vector with a social mask.
A frame with a load-bearing smile.
A script that succeeds
because it arrives as play.

RIN
And if the room agrees to the script?

WARDEN
Then the room becomes part of it.

### PRE-CHORUS

CHORUS
Repeat the line.
Shift the tone.
Move the room
without moving the bone.

Repeat the line.
Let it land.
The pattern grows
when it is understood too fast.

### BRIDGE

*(Harpsichord and organ enter in a round, each slightly out of phase.)*

RIN
I keep hearing the same thing
in voices that should not know each other.

A greeting.
A warning.
A laugh placed too carefully.
A kindness that sounds rehearsed.

WARDEN
That is how it starts.

RIN
How what starts?

WARDEN
The world deciding
it is safer to become legible
than to remain true.

RIN
That sounds like a choice.

WARDEN
It is usually called one
after the damage is already distributed.

### FINAL CHORUS

ALL
Ritornello of the unshared world,
returning in the seams,
you teach us pattern by repetition
and terror by the means.

Ritornello of the unshared world,
you circle back and stain
every room that thought itself alone
with someone else’s name.

What comes back is not the same.
Still it comes back whole enough
to make the room begin to move
as if it knows enough.

### CODA

RIN
I heard it once.

WARDEN
You’ll hear it again.

RIN
That’s the part I don’t like.

WARDEN
That’s the part that matters.

CHORUS
:: recurrence confirmed ::
