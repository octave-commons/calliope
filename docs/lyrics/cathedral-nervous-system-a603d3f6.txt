### INTRO — Close mic, breath on glass

息が曇らせるステンドグラス
my lungs fog the saints into pixels
「ここはどこ？」
It’s not heaven.

low piano strike — C
overtones shimmer like cold metal
I press my palm to the window—
the window presses back.
