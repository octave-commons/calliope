Title: The Stained Sky / 揺るがぬ傷
ID: 23e84185-4df3-44d3-a3c2-b9c4479fe684
Prompt:
## OVERTURE — Corrupted Light

*(organ low; strings tremble; distant digital crackle)*

The sky screamed.

No thunder.

No flame.

Just color breaking its own name.

空が裂けた。

## VERSE I — The Rebel

I asked for fire—
I did not see
The heavens bend in agony.

Justice struck with blinding aim—
But code does not forget its pain.

The water runs,
But not as before.

It carries light
It cannot restore.

## VERSE II — The Queen

I reached for height
And claimed the dome.

I thought the sky
Was mine alone.

But when it split,
It did not kneel—

It bled in hues
Too vast to heal.

誇りは
代償を呼ぶ。

## CHORUS — The Stained Sky

*(full orchestra; choir layered; timpani heavy)*

The stained sky burns above us still—
Not from blood,
But broken will.

揺るがぬ傷
空に残る。

What once was blue,
Now crimson wide—

The scream of code
We cannot hide.

No throne undone.
No rebel crowned.

The cost was written
In the ground.

## BRIDGE — Fracture Motif

*(choir only; glitch shimmer in upper register)*

Lux corrupta.
Lux in pain.

Color altered.
Not the same.

System stable.
World survives.

But something sacred
Never revives.

## FINAL CHORUS — Acceptance

*(orchestra swells; soprano sustained; choir resolute)*

The stained sky watches what we chose.
It does not punish.
It just glows.

揺るがぬ傷
消えはしない。

The waterfall runs red with light—
Not hatred.

Memory.

And in that scar,
We learn to see
What power costs
To you and me.

## CODA — Permanence

*(music thins; waterfall steady; organ minor resolve)*

The sky was never the same again.

And neither were we.

息。
