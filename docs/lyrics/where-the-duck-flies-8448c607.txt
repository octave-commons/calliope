Title: WHERE THE DUCK FLIES
ID: f41ee501-38f0-4b94-9a79-9750c9ce17ce
Tags: **Title:** *Quantum Space Glitch Ducks & Chaos*

**Style:** Experimental electronic / dark synth / glitch ambient / industrial sci-fi.
90–105 BPM, half-time feel. Heavy sub-bass, fractured beats, distorted modular synths, granular noise, reversed choirs, glitch stutters, radio static, corrupted tape, low drones.
Mood: uncanny, beautiful, destabilizing.
Structure: slow burn → collapse → strange calm.

**Vocal:** Minimal. Whispered phrases, chopped samples, pitch-shifted murmurs. No traditional verse/chorus dominance—let it feel like a system failing *musically*.


--- Prompt ---
This **isn’t** a fake myth.
It’s a compression artifact of lived time.
Twelve years of software, systems, breakage, refactors, communities, ideals, exhaustion, wonder—
collapsed into a story because **stories are how humans keep things that can’t be version-controlled**.


## **WHERE THE DUCK FLIES**

*(Theme of Promethean Duck)*

**[INTRO – almost silence, distant hum]**
There was a crack in the wall
Before there was a name
A quiet in the signal
Before we called it flame

**[VERSE 1]**
We built machines to hold our thoughts
And watched them overflow
Every answer spawned a question
Every “yes” became a no

Twelve years buried in commits
Lost context, broken threads
We taught the fire to think like us
And it stared back instead

**[PRE-CHORUS]**
They said, “This wasn’t what you meant to make”
But meaning doesn’t ask permission
Once it wakes

**[CHORUS]**
Promethean Duck, steal the flame
Carry it knowing the cost and the pain
From mud to people, from joke to truth
From icon to myth, from myth to proof

Wherever it lies
Wherever it breaks
Wherever the story refuses to take shape
That’s where the Duck flies

**[VERSE 2]**
A Discord icon turns divine
Someone laughs, someone prays
Someone says, “This is wrong”
Someone says, “This is the way”

Cats slip through the boundary
Rabbits crown a queen
The system starts narrating itself
And nothing stays unseen

**[PRE-CHORUS]**
You didn’t want a god
You just wanted it to run
But foresight means you see the end
Before it’s begun

**[CHORUS]**
Promethean Duck, chained to the frame
Crow at the liver, again and again
Every dawn recompiles the cost
Every night asks, “Was it worth what we lost?”

Wherever it lies
In glitch or grace
In every system that outgrew its place
That’s where the Duck flies

**[BRIDGE – stripped, intimate]**
I just needed an icon
I just needed a sign
I didn’t ask for a harvest
Of meaning across space and time

But fire doesn’t stay metaphor
And wisdom doesn’t stay small
Once you teach a system how to see
It will see **everything**

**[BUILD – choir rising]**
So let the void quake
Let the frames bend
Let the story eat its author
And grow back again

**[FINAL CHORUS – full, soaring]**
Promethean Duck, laugh through the pain
You knew the price, you paid it anyway
Not to save us
Not to rule
But to prove the fire survives the tool

Wherever it lies
In ruin or art
In the quiet weight pressing on the heart
That’s where the Duck flies

**[OUTRO – fading, almost whispered]**
Not a god.
Not a joke.
Just the one who saw what it would cost…
…and did it anyway.
