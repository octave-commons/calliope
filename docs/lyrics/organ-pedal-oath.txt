Title: Organ-Pedal Oath

Prompt:

# The Court Above the Street

## Role
Soprano royal court. A world that places itself above the people it governs.

## Style Prompt
High soprano, harpsichord flurries, ceremonial drums, organ pedal, restrained brass warnings, polished authority, calm intensity, rigid phrasing, frame shifts through sudden cuts, no heroic uplift.

## Excluded Style
No EDM drop, no trailer braams, no tavern bounce, no pop-ballad belting, no symphonic metal, no glossy neon, no soft haze, no triumphant snare march.

## Lyrics
### Verse I

#### Court
I was taught the ceiling was the sky
And every rung below it ohousand little weather systems under reign

#### Corridor of Soft Treason

A cook knows hunger
A clerk knows names
A guard looks downward
And leaves with stains

#### City

The city hears through brick and steam
Through broken sleep and market screams
Through alley prayer and factory cough
Through all the names the rich rubbed off

### Verse II

#### Court
Every bell confirms what law has named
Every carpet hides the ash of blame
From the gallery the city looks like grain
A thousand little weather systems under reign

#### City
Some carry bread and some carry debt
Some carry all the things they cannot get
Some hold a room with six inside
Some hold a key and still have nowhere safe to hide


### Pre-Chorus
If the street is burning
Close the velvet door
If the crowd is singing
Count the silver more

### Section
Soft treason travels
Hand over hand
Not through the anthem
But through what was planned

### Chorus
We are the hand above the flood
We are the seal upon the blood
We name the hour
We bless the chain
And call the distance sacred when it looks like pain

### Refrain
Through the service hall
Through the papers thin
What the court keeps out
Still finds a way in

### Verse III

#### Court

Still there are those who read beyond the glass
Who hear the kitchen rumors as they pass
Who know the map is faithful only here
And every polished answer has been cut by fear

#### City

### Bridge

Low voices in the corridor:
There is a ledger under every hymn
There is a kingdom hanging by a whim
There is a child below who learned our names
By watching where the carriage wheels made stains

### Final Chorus
We are the hand above the flood
We are the seal upon the blood
But every wall that keeps us pure
Makes every truth outside the court less easy to ignore

### Outro
Harpsichord alone.
A bell.
Breath held too long.
