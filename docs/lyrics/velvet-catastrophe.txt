Title: Velvet Catastrophe / ベルベット・カタストロフ
ID: ac61ec24-02ba-4eac-8f7f-6350fd1719a0
Prompt:
## OVERTURE — Crimson Curtain

*(harpsichord racing; strings tremolo)*

Welcome, my darling,
to velvet decay.

赤い幕が上がる。

The stage is immaculate—
the ending pre-paid.

## VERSE 1 — Luxury of Loss

I sell you salvation in crystal and lace,
perfumed catastrophe dressed up as grace.

Misfortune gilded in glitter and wine,
tragedy posed like it’s fate by design.

Drink from the chalice—
forget what you knew.

忘れて。

## PRE-CHORUS — Invitation

*(strings climbing; timpani rumble)*

Hush now, hush now,
the orchestra swells.

Every paradise
comes with a bell.

One toll for wonder,
one toll for sin—

Open the door.
Step in.

## CHORUS — Velvet Catastrophe

*(full orchestral surge; soprano at peak)*

Velvet catastrophe—
bow to the queen!

Golden disaster in porcelain sheen.

Clap for the ruin,
cheer for the fall—

Isn’t it beautiful?
Isn’t it all?

ベルベットの破滅
甘く囁く。

Velvet catastrophe—
kneel if you please.

Your chains are roses,
your cage is keys.

## VERSE 2 — Commodity Heaven

Happiness packaged in ribbons and fear,
buy one illusion—regret disappears.

Morality melts in theatrical light,
wrong becomes dazzling, dark becomes bright.

I whisper: “Dream.”
I murmur: “Stay.”

夢を見て。

## BRIDGE — Cabaret of Consent

*(tempo dips; harpsichord solo; whisper)*

Be quiet now.
Be good.

Let the spectacle decide what it should.

You wanted a myth?
You wanted a throne?

Every empire
eats its own.

*(choir enters, low chant beneath soprano sustain)*

Lux in corruptione—
truth in disguise.

## FINAL CHORUS — Exalted Collapse

*(strings frantic; organ blazing; glitch fracture accents)*

Velvet catastrophe—
rise and applaud!

Cathedral fire with mechanical god.

Salvation lacquered,
hollow inside—

Dance on the edge
where angels lied.

ベルベットの破滅
美しく燃える。

Velvet catastrophe—
believe what you see.

If you call it destiny,
it can’t be me.

## STINGER — Curtain Drop

*(orchestra cuts sharply; single harpsichord note)*

Applause.

息。
