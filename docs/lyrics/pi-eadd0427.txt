Π

Projection.

Pattern given form.

If η is the field
and μ is the step,

Π is the shape that remains after repeated steps in a field.

---

In the Gate:

η tilts the lattice.
μ moves through it.
Π is the path worn into the stone.

Not a single action.

A trajectory.

When Duct always stands left — that’s μ.
When “left” becomes moral geometry — that’s Π.

When variance is chosen once — μ.
When resilience becomes architecture — Π.

Π is narrative crystallized from behavior.

---

Systems love Π.

Patterns are compressible.
Patterns are modelable.
Patterns are predictable.

That’s why Axiom hunts Π.

Not μ.
μ is noisy.
Π is leverage.

If they can map your projection,
they can precompute your future.

---

But Π can branch.

If μ varies under η,
then Π is not a line.

It is a tree.

Not a single trajectory.
A probability cloud.

And that

is what makes domination expensive.

---

Truth does not eliminate Π.

Truth accumulates it.

Every breath (息),
every field (η),
every action (μ),

projects something.

The question is not
whether Π forms.

The question is:

Is it a spear,

or a forest?
