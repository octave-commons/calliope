nobody measuring the noise floor.

reposted creed,

while you plagiarize feed.

Signal-to-noise,
but you don’t touch the math,
You just label what you like
as “truth” on your path.

SNR low, but your ego loud,

from a recycled cloud.

If your model’s brittle,
don’t blame the static,
You tuned it for applause,
not the signal analytic.
