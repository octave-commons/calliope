Title: Corridor Seepage
ID: 31ddc907-1bc6-4303-ad10-60347f5fd7d6
Prompt:
# The City Hears Everything

## Role
The city as living frame. The crowd, the struggle, the unequal room, with corridor seepage and outsider contact at the edges.

## Style Prompt
Layered chorus, urgent pulse, street wind textures, low percussion, bowed strings, crowded call-and-response, cheap-phone crowd grain, motion through pressure not speed. Hushed ensemble, plucked strings cut by buffer clicks, service-hall footsteps, low strings under breath, murmured prose, close-mic secrecy. Bright but restrained threshold piece, delicate chimes, hand percussion, plucked motifs, pauses that feel like negotiation, polite glitches, open harmonies.

## Excluded Style
No EDM drop, no trailer braams, no tavern bounce, no pop-ballad belting, no symphonic metal, no glossy neon, no soft haze, no triumphant snare march, no festival build, no lush Hollywood swell, no generic cyberpunk neon, no retro 80s gloss, no vaporwave drift, no outrun sheen, no mainstream dark-pop chorus, no overdesigned fairy whimsy.

## Lyrics

### Verse I

#### City
The city learns by smoke and rain
By bootstep rhythm, siren strain
By window arguments at dawn
By who is fed and who moves on

#### Corridor
A cook knows hunger
A clerk knows names
A guard looks downward
And leaves with stains


### Verse II

#### City
Some carry bread and some carry debt
Some carry all the things they cannot get
Some hold a room with six inside
Some hold a key and still have nowhere safe to hide

#### Threshold
Coin on a counter
Light in a crate
A room says welcome
But counts the plates


### Pre-Chorus

#### City
Roofline to roofline
Signal to skin
Need outruns mercy
Every time it has to win


### Section

#### Threshold
You offer structure
They answer, cost
You offer patience
They answer, time is lost


### Chorus

#### City
The city hears everything
Coins, commands, and wedding rings
Bootsteps, bargains, fear, and rain
Nothing here is small when pressed through pain


### Refrain

#### Threshold
At the threshold market
Meaning changes hands
Not all who understand it
Can afford where understanding lands


### Bridge

#### City
Crowd divided, crowd one body
Hands in conflict, hands in trade
Every kindness hits a limit
Every shelter has a grade

#### Corridor
There is a ledger under every hymn
There is a kingdom hanging by a whim
There is a child below who learned our names
By watching where the carriage wheels made stains


### Final Chorus

#### City
The city keeps the voices when the mouths go still
The wind remembers what the law will never will


### Outro

#### Stage Direction
Street-wind, train brake, low server hum, voices thinning but never fully gone.
