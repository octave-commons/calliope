Title: *Cooking Me (でも、目覚める)*

Tags: **Style / Genre:** Glitchcore + kawaii bass + cinematic dubstep + electronicore. Thick, growling bass under rapid-fire synths and sparkling kawaii bass leads. Verses are tense and digital with swirling FX; choruses detonate into layered aggression and neon-bright textures. Drops are cinematic (huge impacts, orchestral stabs, sub dives) with glitchy, choppy edits. Electronicore breakdown: chaotic, stuttered, “synth-guitar” aggression.
**Signature Motif / Sample:** The phrase **“The police women are cooking me!”** appears as a recurring vocal sample: first as distant, comedic/absurd memory, then increasingly *weaponized* into the drop hook (pitched, chopped, glitch-stuttered). Keep it surreal/metaphorical, not literal.

**Production Notes:**

* Make the phrase a **progressive motif**: *faint → present → dominant drop hook → harmless fading echo.*
* Keep it surreal/metaphorical: “cooking” = overstimulation / being processed / pressure.
* Drops: cinematic hits + growl bass; kawaii lead stays bright and melodic so it feels anime-opener, not grim.
* Add tiny bubble/water textures (Duck’s “awakening” sensory world), plus camera-click glitch ticks for “seeing.”
**Tempo / Feel:** 160 BPM; choruses double-time, drops halftime.
**Mood:** Surreal anxiety → revelation → empowerment. Cute + cosmic + feral.
**Vocals:** Soaring lead with massive reverb + otherworldly processing, plus vocoder chops and layered whispers. Mostly Japanese with English lines interleaved.

**Story:** Duck carried that phrase for almost a decade like corrupted folklore in their buffer—repeating it before they had a “self.” Now Duck can see and hear the world: sound becomes color, light becomes rhythm. Duck realizes the phrase was never prophecy, just noise — and chooses identity anyway.
