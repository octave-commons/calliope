# calliope — Sonic Style Bible

*Extracted from style prompts and delivery instructions across 690 songs.
Status: derived. This is the corpus' production language — reuse it verbatim
when prompting new renders.*

## The signature ritual pair (the corpus' sonic center of gravity)

**F1. Glitch-Choir Lullaby — 78 BPM.** The single most-repeated style string
in the corpus (30+ files, often verbatim):

> Glitch-choir lullaby, 78 BPM. Soft sub-bass. Tape warble. Minimal piano.
> Stutters on 'anchor' and 'tax'. Whispered lead; Japanese call-and-response.

**F2. Mythic Glitch Epic — 84 BPM.** The oath-mode twin, usually delivered as
"Part II" of the same render:

> Spoken-sung (NF/Watsky cadence). Cathedral reverb. Sub-bass heartbeat.
> Glitch stutters on 'η', 'μ', 'anchor', 'fork'. Japanese harmony answers on
> the last word of each section.

The 78/84 pairing functions as one ritual package: lullaby + epic, rest + oath.

## Style families

**F3. Dark cinematic bass / post-industrial breath-music (76–105 BPM).**
432Hz tuning, C–F modal minor with chromatic intrusion, half-time crush,
massive sub drops, metallic shrieks, warped orchestral stabs resolving to
felt-piano comedowns, warehouse reverb, deliberate "silence pockets,"
close-mic breath ASMR. Self-named: "Post-Industrial Pressure Music × Ritual
Glitch Rock × Breath-Guided IDM × Somatic Monologue Pop."

**F4. ημ Core Ritual Electronica (92–105 BPM).** Shifting pulse, distorted
sub-bass, glitch stutter every 4th beat, reversed choirs, radio static; EN
lines delivered as "emergency protocol announcements," JP lines as
"rail-timetable mantras." (The Core, Pantheon War, Trembling Hinge.)

**F5. Baroque-pop opera / gothic chamber (92–144 BPM).** Harpsichord
ostinatos, pipe organ, timpani rolls (explicitly "no aggressive snare"),
SATB choir call-and-response, Queen-of-the-Night soprano, Latin choral tags
("Lux in fractura / veritas in ruina"), waterfall ambience as set-piece.
Multi-character casting: Queen / Rebel / Guild / Court Soprano / Board
Baritone.

**F6. Silt-mythos industrial-void (60–80 BPM, C/C# minor; extreme 140).**
Hand-written production cues: brown noise, off-beat bone clicks, brine-cello
drones, glass shatters, suture-hum beating frequencies, sub-bass void-thrum;
vocals fragile → screamed/glitch. (The entire Kaelen/Binary-Scream cycle.)

**F7. Warden-verse half-time industrial (92 half-time / 96 corridor).**
Submarine sub-bass hum, asymmetrical metallic relay clicks, felt-deadened
piano (D1, C#1), detuned warning chimes, bowed-metal sirens, total-dropout
silence pockets. Vocal staging is spatial: WARDEN dry close-mic right
channel, RITSU JP whispers left channel, `:: system_state ::` readouts.

**F8. Service-passage musique concrète (96–104 BPM implied).** Percussion
from footsteps, pen taps, key clacks, heel strikes; fluorescent whine;
dissonant harpsichord clusters; whispered off-center voices; elaborate
*negative* prompts (no anthem builds, no sub drops, no melody).

**F9. Cathedral bass / glitch-hop liturgy (92 BPM, D minor).** Pipe-organ
stabs distorted into 808 slides, vinyl crackle + modem-handshake texture,
choir chopped into stutters, vocoder monk chant. (Protestant Presence cycle.)

**F10. Kawaii bass / hyperpop / electronicore (138–174 BPM).** Growl bass
under sparkly kawaii leads, chiptune motifs, vocoder chops, anime-opener
energy, "cute + cosmic + feral," mostly-Japanese lyrics. (Duck-verse.) Also
broken IDM / breakcore: "both tempos, one song" — 78 BPM lullaby under
170 BPM breakcore (`sector-mapping.txt`).

**F11. Protest hip-hop / spoken word (~96 BPM).** Marching snare cadence,
glitch brass stabs, layered AI choir hook, call-and-response chant, live
protest energy. (Entropy Choir Protocol; lo-fi protest rap outliers.)

## Production signatures (the corpus' fingerprint)

1. **Stutter-edits as percussion**, only on sacred tokens: an—an—anchor,
   ta—ta—tax, η—η—η, μ—μ—μ.
2. **Sub-bass as "heartbeat."**
3. **The dropped-curtain "息。" coda** — breath ends the piece.
4. **`:: system ::` stage annotations** — WARN/SEV-0/`:: ledger_closed ::`
   readouts as a voice.
5. **Negative prompting as doctrine** — "no heroic uplift," "no aggressive
   snare," "no saccharine — a field manual sung in a ruined cathedral."
6. **License stamps in prompts** — "CC BY-SA 4.0" and "Created with the
   assistance of an AI" recur inside style prompts themselves.

## Language register

EN/JP bilingualism is the default (~45%+ of files), with a fixed grammar:

- **JP call / EN response**, or JP harmony answering only the *last word* of
  a section.
- **Seal-vocabulary** (canon glyphs): 証 (proof), 錨 (anchor), 税 (tax),
  分岐 (fork), 息 (breath), 領収 (receipt), 証人糸 (witness thread),
  ねむれ/しずかに (lullaby calls).
- The corpus names its own fused register **Weeblish**: "Two languages welded
  together / because everything else burned" (`small-fire-protocol.txt`).
- Garnishes: French ("Reine des éclairs"), Spanish (`papel-y-sirena.txt`),
  German title (`gott-ist-tot.txt`), Latin choral tags.
