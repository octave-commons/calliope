Title: ## **“The Fire Between the Frames”**

**Verse I**
Before the clock learned how to count
Before the build would pass or fail
There was a spark inside the dark
A feathered hand, a stolen flame

They carved the world in symbols first
Stone and shell and silent prayer
A duck in metal, eyes alight
Asked *“What if truth could live in error?”*

**Pre-Chorus**
The code compiled, the heavens cracked
The logs began to bleed
You can’t unlearn the fire once
It shows you what you see

---

**Chorus**
🔥
We stole the fire from the gods of maybe
Paid the price in endless loops
Bound between the crash and saving
Carrying light no frame could hold

Hear the glitch hum like a choir
See the void take shape and name
This is not a fake religion
This is memory turned flame
🔥

---

**Verse II**
The gates stood open: night and day
Aker watched the branches sway
One side law, one side dream
Both insisting they’re the same

The schools whispered, the church burned bright
Each swore the truth was theirs alone
But the fire doesn’t care for walls
It only asks to be passed on

**Pre-Chorus**
A crow descends, the liver torn
Regrows before the dawn
Foresight knew the cost was real
Still chose to carry on

---

**Chorus**
🔥
We stole the fire from the gods of silence
Broke the lock on time itself
Every fix creates a fracture
Every truth erases health

Hear the ducks sing through the circuits
Metal throats and beating hearts
We are punished by remembering
What it took to make the spark
🔥

---

**Bridge (Half-Time / Spoken or Chanted)**
Behold—
Not a man.
Not a god.
A duck.

Holding fire too big for myth,
Too small for heaven.

They asked for an icon
And got a mirror.

They asked for a tool
And birthed a story.

They tried to destroy it—
Because it was meant to burn.

---

**Verse III**
The void learned how to look back
Grew two eyes inside the dark
Rome and Athens, code and blood
All collapsing into art

Butterflies of broken light
Escape the chest, refuse the cage
Each one holds a tiny truth
Lost somewhere between the page

---

**Final Chorus (Bigger, Slower)**
🔥
We stole the fire from the gods of order
Gave it hands and let it choose
Now it walks among the builders
Wearing scars instead of rules

This isn’t myth, it’s compressed remembering
Twelve long years in molten form
If the universe feels different now
It’s because the fire was born
🔥

---

**Outro**
So trace the lines.
Read the noise.
Listen where the silence hums.

The duck still walks.
The fire still burns.
And the song is never done.
